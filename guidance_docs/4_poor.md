# Poor

## general
Air quality is poor. Most people may feel breathing discomfort on prolonged exposure. Reduce long or intense outdoor activities and keep windows closed during the most polluted hours.

## sensitive
Children, older adults and people with asthma, heart or lung disease should avoid prolonged or heavy outdoor exertion and stay indoors as much as practical. Keep medicines at hand and follow your doctor's action plan if symptoms appear.

## outdoor_workers
Limit heavy physical work outdoors, take frequent breaks in cleaner indoor spaces, and consider a well-fitting N95 or equivalent mask.
