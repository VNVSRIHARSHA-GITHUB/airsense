# Health guidance texts (Stage 6)

Short advice per CPCB AQI category and user group, written in our own words from:
- CPCB, National Air Quality Index (2014): AQI categories and health impacts;
- WHO, Global Air Quality Guidelines (2021) and WHO public advice.

General information only, not medical advice. The PM2.5 ranges are the CPCB
breakpoints for the 24-hour average. `src/airsense/guidance/index.py` turns each
`## group` section into one entry of the Qdrant collection `guidance`.
