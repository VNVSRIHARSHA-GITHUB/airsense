# Severe

## general
Air quality is severe: it affects healthy people and seriously affects those with existing diseases. Avoid all outdoor physical activity, stay indoors with windows closed, use an air purifier if available, and wear a well-fitting N95 or equivalent mask if you must go out.

## sensitive
Children, older adults and people with asthma, heart or lung disease should remain indoors and avoid any exertion. Seek medical help promptly if breathing difficulty, chest pain, dizziness or unusual tiredness occurs.

## outdoor_workers
Outdoor work should be minimised or stopped. If unavoidable, wear a well-fitting N95 or equivalent mask, keep shifts short and rest in clean indoor air.
