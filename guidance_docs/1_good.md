# Good

## general
Air quality is good and poses little or no risk. A good time for outdoor activities, exercise and airing your home by opening windows.

## sensitive
Little or no risk, also for children, older adults and people with asthma, heart or lung disease. Normal activities can continue; keep usual medicines at hand as always.

## outdoor_workers
No special precautions are needed for outdoor work or exercise.
