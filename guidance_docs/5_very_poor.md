# Very Poor

## general
Air quality is very poor and may cause respiratory illness on prolonged exposure. Avoid outdoor exercise, keep windows and doors closed, and use an air purifier indoors if available. A well-fitting N95 or equivalent mask helps when going out.

## sensitive
Children, older adults and people with asthma, heart or lung disease should stay indoors and avoid physical exertion. Keep medicines at hand and seek medical advice if breathing difficulty, chest pain or unusual tiredness occurs.

## outdoor_workers
Avoid heavy outdoor work where possible; reschedule tasks to cleaner hours, wear a well-fitting N95 or equivalent mask, and take breaks indoors.
