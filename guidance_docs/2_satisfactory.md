# Satisfactory

## general
Air quality is acceptable. Most people can continue outdoor activities as usual.

## sensitive
Unusually sensitive people may feel minor breathing discomfort. If you notice coughing or shortness of breath, take breaks and reduce long or intense outdoor exertion.

## outdoor_workers
Outdoor work can continue normally. Take regular breaks and drink water; sensitive workers should watch for breathing discomfort.
