# Moderate

## general
Air quality is moderate. Healthy people are usually not affected, but consider shortening long or intense outdoor exercise, especially near busy roads.

## sensitive
People with asthma, heart or lung disease may feel breathing discomfort. Reduce prolonged or heavy outdoor exertion, keep reliever medicines at hand, and prefer indoor activities for children and older adults during the most polluted hours.

## outdoor_workers
Take more frequent breaks and avoid the busiest traffic periods where possible. Workers with heart or lung conditions should limit heavy exertion outdoors.
