    How to read this page

    PM2.5 are fine particles in the air, small enough to reach deep into the lungs. Values are the average over the last 24 hours, in micrograms per cubic metre (µg/m³), as used by India's official Air Quality Index (AQI).

    CategoryPM2.5 (24-hour average)Possible health impact

    The forecast shows the expected 24-hour average for each of the next 24 hours. It changes slowly because each new hour replaces only one of the 24 hours being averaged.

    Why this forecast lists the inputs that pushed the forecast up or down the most, in µg/m³.

    Notes appear when some input data was missing; the forecast is still made, but may be less accurate. If no forecast is shown, the latest measurement is too old or missing.

    Measurements: Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB), via data.gov.in. Weather: Open-Meteo.com (CC BY 4.0). Forecasts are estimates; follow official health advisories.
